Challenges I had

I spent a long time getting the "island navigation" to work, and displaying the image buttons correctly on all devices and aspect ratios.

To fix this I adjusted the postions back and forth, changing the margin and padding on the elements, and instead of using a static # of px for the padding/ margin, I used a percentage.

Simalarly, I also had issues getting the sizing right for text sizes.

What I learned

Size ratio

I had to learn how to work with sizing the elements correctly so it looks right on different devices. This meant instead of using something like "20px" for distance and sizing. I used a percentage.

Ex: max-width: 70%;

70% percent means the box that this element is in weather it's a "div" or just the text on it's own. The max-width will be 70% percent of that.
